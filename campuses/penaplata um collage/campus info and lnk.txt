About UM Penaplata
Sometime in the early part of 1950, a group of Samal pioneers, led by former District Supervisor Florencio Valera, requested the administration of the University Mindanao (Davao District), formerly Mindanao Colleges to open a branch at Samal particularly in Peñaplata to cater to incoming high school students.
