1.DR. GUILLERMO P. TORRES, JR.
Chairman
Member - Nomination Committee

Dr. Guillermo P. Torres, Jr., Ed.D., is a respected Filipino educator, media executive, businessman, and civic leader. He currently serves as Chairman of the Board and President of several institutions, including GET Holdings, Inc., the University of Mindanao (UM), UM Broadcasting Network UMBN), Mount Apo Science Foundation, Inc., and other key ventures in education, media, real estate, hospitality, and healthcare across Mindanao and Metro Manila.

Dr. Torres holds a Doctor of Education in Educational Management from the University of Mindanao (2004), and was conferred a Doctor of Philosophy in Commercial Science, Honoris Causa, by the John Dewey University Consortium of America in New Jersey, USA. He also earned a Master of Business Administration from Ohio University in Athens, Ohio (1981), and a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from De La Salle University, Manila (1975). His early education includes a high school diploma from Port Huron High School in Michigan, USA (1971), and elementary studies at Don Bosco Technical Institute in Makati (1967).

He was conferred the rank of Lieutenant Colonel in the Philippine Army Reserve, serving as Battalion Commander of the 1st Signal PAARU, Ready Reserve Battalion, ARESCOM. He is also active in civic and professional organizations, currently serving as President of the Davao Association of Colleges and Universities Network (DACUN),  American Chamber Mindanao, Datu Bago Awards Inc., and past President of the Rotary Club of West Davao (RY 2001–2002). He is a member of various local and international business and sports clubs.

Among his numerous accolades is the Datu Bago Award (2011), Davao City’s highest honor. He is the lead awardee 2017 by the Davao JCI Senate. He also has lifetime achievement award 2025 by the Kapisanan ng mga Brodkaster ng Pilipinas (KBP), and the Lasallian Achiever in Media Award from De La Salle University (2004).

Born on August 17, 1955, he is the son of the late Atty. Guillermo E. Torres and Dolores Pilpil. He is married to Elizabeth S. Dizon, with whom he has two children, Paolo Roberto and Maria Isabelle.

President Torres became a member of the Board of Trustees on November 1, 1986.


2.ATTY. EDWARD ANTHONY C. GO
First Vice Chairman
Member - Audit Committee

ATTY. EDWARD ANTHONY C. GO
Edward Anthony Go is a Filipino business executive born on December 27, 1972, in Davao City, Philippines. He is married to Leigh T. Go.

Mr. Go obtained his Bachelor of Laws degree from the University of Mindanao and became a registered lawyer in 2012.  He acquired his Bachelor of Business Administration degree with a double major in Finance and Marketing and minor in Economics from Simon Fraser University in Burnaby, British Columbia, Canada.

He is currently the Vice President for Operations of Mintrade Corporation, a company engaged in the manufacture and trading of wood products, headquartered at 888 Mintrade Drive, Davao City. He also serves in the same capacity at Mintrade Shipping Lines, Inc., which operates in inter-island cargo shipping.

In addition, Atty. Go is the Managing Director of Em-eukal Philippines, Inc., a company involved in the manufacturing of confectionery products.

With extensive experience across multiple industries—including law, manufacturing, shipping, and international business—Atty. Go plays a key role in the development and management of several business ventures in the Philippines.

He joined the University of Mindanao as member of the Board of Trustees on September 25, 1998

4.DR. PEDRO B. SAN JOSE
Treasurer
Chairman - Renumeration/Compensation Committee

DR. PEDRO B. SAN JOSE
Mr. Pedro “Pete” San Jose, a native of Arayat, Pampanga, is a respected educator, civic leader, and public servant. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Education from the Far Eastern University in Manila and earned his Master of Arts from the University of Mindanao and the Harvardian University in Davao City in July 1953.


Throughout his distinguished career, Mr. San Jose has held various leadership roles in community and civic organizations. He currently serves as President of the New Christ the King Cathedral Building Commission, President of Region XI Government Retirees Association, Region XI Home-for-the-Aged (Tagum Friends of the Home), and the Davao del Norte Government Retirees Association. He is also Vice Chairman for Council Administration of the Boy Scouts of the Philippines, Davao del Norte Council.


Mr. San Jose’s political involvement includes serving as Barangay Councilor of Magugpo, Tagum (1964–1968) and as Provincial Board Member of Davao del Norte (1987–1995). He was instrumental in crafting ordinances establishing the College Provincial Scholarship Grant—the first of its kind in the Philippines—and the Provincial Subdivision Grant, which provided residential lots for lowly employees.


He has also represented Davao del Norte in numerous athletic and sports events, including the Palarong Pambansa, National PRISAA, Mindanao Sports Summits, and SMRAA meets across the country.
He was also awarded by the Holy See a Papal Award Pro Ecclesia et Pontifice (for the Church and Pope), the highest medal awarded to the laity by the Pope dated February 2017. He was also awarded Solemn Investiture dated April 13, 2017, at the Christ the King Cathedral.


A recipient of numerous distinctions, Mr. San Jose has received 8 trophies and 37 plaques of recognition and appreciation for his exceptional contributions to education, church, and community development. In 1999, the UM gym in Tagum Gymnasium was named Pedro  B. San Jose Gymnasium in his honor, and in the same year, he was recognized nationally as a Huwarang Pamilyang Pilipino Awardee.


He joined the University of Mindanao as a member of the Board of Trustees in 2000.

8.ATTY. JESUS MELCHOR V. QUITAIN
Trustee
Member - Audit Committee, Member - Renumeration/Compensation Committee
ATTY. JESUS MELCHOR V. QUITAIN
Jesus Melchor “Boy” Vega Quitain, born on January 6, 1945, is a seasoned Filipino lawyer and public servant known for his longstanding service in both local and national government. He served as Chief Presidential Legal Counsel and Special Assistant to the President during Duterte’s term, the position he held from 2016 to 2022. He is widely credited as the principal speechwriter behind many of President Duterte’s most notable speeches, including his inaugural and State of the Nation addresses. 


A native of Davao City, Quitain earned his law degree from San Beda College of Law in 1970.  He is also a member of Upsilon Sigma Phi in his college days. After being admitted to the bar, he entered private law practice in 1971 and later became Dean of the College of Law at the University of Mindanao.


His public service career began in 2001, when then Davao City Mayor Rodrigo Duterte appointed him as City Legal Officer. He subsequently served as City Councilor, City Administrator, and Resident Ombudsman of Davao City, for which he was recognized as the Outstanding Resident Ombudsman of the Philippines in 2008. He also became the first National President of the Association of Resident Ombudsman in Government Agencies and served as President of the Rotary Club of South Davao.


In national government, Quitain held several key roles, including Officer-in-Charge of the Office of the Special Assistant to the President, before being appointed Chief Presidential Legal Counsel in 2021.


He is the father of former Davao City Vice Mayor Atty. J. Melchor Bumpus Quitain Jr. and Dr. Grace Ann Quitain – Pecson.


He is married to Anita Bumpus Quitain who was a Commissioner of the Social Security System Commission for a period of eight years. 

He joined the University of Mindanao as member of the Board of Trustees on June 1, 2005.

10.ATTY. JESUS DUREZA
Trustee

ATTY. JESUS DUREZA
Jesus "Jess" Gestuveo Dureza, 77 years old, is a lawyer, consultant, journalist and public servant from Davao City. He served as Presidential Adviser on the Peace Process under former President Rodrigo Roa Duterte from June 30, 2016 to November 2018, and was the Founding Chairman of the Advocacy MindaNow Foundation, a peace and development advocacy group in Mindanao. 


He has held several key government positions, including Chairman of the Mindanao Development Authority, Chairman of the Government Peace Negotiating Panel for Talks with the Moro Islamic Liberation Front, and Presidential Assistant for Mindanao. He was first elected as Representative of the 1st District of Davao City in 1987, and was re-elected in 1992. 


Atty. Dureza was born on December 24, 1947, in Guimbal, Iloilo. His father, Martin Dureeza, hailed from Janiuay, Iloilo and took part in the local guerrilla resistance against the Japanese Army during World War II. When Jess was one year old, his family migrated to Davao. He finished elementary school as class valedictorian at Guihing Elementary School in Hagonoy in 1959 and graduated with honors from Holy Cross of Digos High School (now Cor Jesu College) in 1963, where he was a classmate of former President Rodrigo Duterte. 


He earned his Bachelor of Arts degree, Cum Laude, from Ateneo de Davao University in 1967, and completed his Bachelor of Laws degree from the same institution in 1972, placing 10th in the 1973 Philippine Bar Examinations.


Before entering politics, Dureza built a distinguished career in journalism. While still a law student, he worked as radio host for RMN Davao and as Davao correspondent for The Manila Times. After finishing law, he became editor of the Mindanao Times, correspondent for the Manila Bulletin, and television presenter of “Brainstorm” in Davao City. He also served as President of the Davao Press Club in 1972.


In government service, he served as Director of the Philippine Coconut Authority (1989 – 1992) under President Corazon Aquino. He was later appointed by President Fidel V. Ramos as Presidential Assistant for Mindanao and Chairman of the Mindanao Economic Development Council in 1998, and also served as Presidential Spokesperson from 1998 to 2000.


During President Gloria Macapagal Arroyo’s administration, he was appointed as Chairman of the GRP Panel for Talks with the Moro Islamic Liberation Front  (2001–2003), Chairman of the Mindanao Economic Development Council (2001–2006), Presidential Adviser on the Peace Process (2006–2008), Press Secretary (2008–2009), Chief Presidential Legal Counsel (2009), and Presidential Adviser on Mindanao (2009–2010). He was later designated as Chairman of the Mindanao Development Authority (2010) and Philippine Signing Minister to BIMP-EAGA.


Dureza has received several prestigious awards, including the Datu Bago Award (1983), the highest honor given to a Davaoeno, and the Presidential Medal of Merit conferred by President Fidel V. Ramos in June 1998. 


He was later appointed Publisher of the Mindanao Times and has been serving as a member of the Board of Trustees of the University of Mindanao since December 14, 2011.



11.DARLENE MAGNOLIA CUSTODIO
Trustee

DARLENE MAGNOLIA CUSTODIO
Darlene Magnolia Ricasa Antonino-Custodio was born on December 26, 1973, in California, United States. She completed her high school education at O.B. Montessori in Manila in 1992 and earned a Bachelor of Science in business management from De La Salle University in 1996. She later pursued a Master’s Degree in Culinary Arts at the prestigious Le Cordon Bleu in London, England.

A former public servant, Antonino-Custodio served as Mayor of General Santos City from 2010 to 2013 and as Representative of South Cotabato’s 1st District from 2007 to 2010. She was affiliated with the NPC-AIM party and held the position of House Deputy Minority Leader during the 13th Congress of the Philippines.

Coming from a prominent political family, she is the daughter of Adelbert and Luwalhati R. Antonino, who both served in key political roles in General Santos and South Cotabato. She is also the granddaughter of former Senators Gaudencio and Magnolia Antonino, and a descendant of an American great-grandfather on her maternal side.

Darlene Antonino-Custodio resides in New Manila, Quezon City. She is married to businessman Benjamin Custodio, and they have two children: Aaron Vincent and Karina Anika. She is a Board of Trustee since July 30, 2010.
