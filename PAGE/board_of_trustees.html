Board of Trustees

Meet the visionary leaders committed to the University of Mindanao’s mission of transformative education and global engagement.

Chairman / President

Dr. Guillermo P. Torres, Jr.

Dr. Guillermo P. Torres, Jr., Ed.D., is a respected Filipino educator, media executive, businessman, and civic leader. He currently serves as Chairman of the Board and President of several institutions, including GET Holdings, Inc., the University of Mindanao (UM), and UM Broadcasting Network.

Dr. Torres holds a Doctor of Education in Educational Management from UM and was conferred an honorary PhD in Commercial Science by the John Dewey University Consortium. He earned his MBA from Ohio University and BSBA from De La Salle University. He is a Lieutenant Colonel in the Philippine Army Reserve and serves as President of the Davao Association of Colleges and Universities Network (DACUN).

First Vice Chairman

Atty. Edward Anthony C. Go

Edward Anthony Go is a Filipino business executive born in Davao City. He obtained his Bachelor of Laws from the University of Mindanao and became a registered lawyer in 2012. He also holds a BBA from Simon Fraser University, Canada.

Atty. Go is currently the Vice President for Operations of Mintrade Corporation and Mintrade Shipping Lines, Inc. He also serves as Managing Director of Em-eukal Philippines, Inc., bringing extensive experience across law, manufacturing, and shipping to the board.

Second Vice Chairman

Mr. Edwin P. Torres

Edwin P. Torres is a marketing professional and academic leader. He earned his BS in Business Administration, majoring in Marketing, from De La Salle University and later graduated from Philippine Christian University.

He began his career at the University of Mindanao in 1983 and has served in various leadership roles within the marketing field, including President of the Junior Philippine Marketing Association (JPMA). He has been a member of the Board of Trustees since 1985.

Treasurer

Dr. Pedro B. San Jose

Mr. Pedro “Pete” San Jose is a respected educator, civic leader, and public servant. He holds a BSE from Far Eastern University and a Master of Arts from the University of Mindanao.

He has served as a Provincial Board Member of Davao del Norte and was instrumental in establishing the country's first Provincial Scholarship Grant. He was awarded the Papal Award *Pro Ecclesia et Pontifice* by the Holy See in 2017 for his exceptional contributions to the church and community. The UM Gymnasium in Tagum is named in his honor.

Trustee / Corporate Secretary

Atty. Jose Anthony Manuel S. Nitorreda

Jose Antonio Manuel S. Nitorreda is a trained lawyer and corporate governance officer. He holds degrees in Arts, Commerce (Accounting), and Law from Ateneo de Davao University.

He has served as Legal Counsel and Corporate Secretary for Davao Doctors Hospital and Davao Doctors College. He brings a combination of legal expertise and healthcare governance experience to his role as Corporate Secretary of the UM Board of Trustees.

Trustee

Atty. Salvador Medialdea

Salvador Medialdea is a prominent Filipino lawyer who served as the Executive Secretary under the presidency of Rodrigo Duterte (2016–2022). He previously headed the Livelihood Corporation (LIVECOR) and served as Presidential Assistant for Political Affairs.

A native of Davao, he played a critical role in supporting national executive decisions and government operations. He joined the UM Board of Trustees in early 2024.

Trustee

Hon. Pilar C. Braga

Councilor Pilar Braga is the Chairperson of the Davao City Government's Committee on Education, Science, and Technology. She is a staunch advocate for women's participation in governance and grassroots development.

With 16 ordinances and 149 resolutions to her name, she has significantly influenced education and cultural initiatives in Davao City. She has been a valued member of the UM Board since 2017.

Trustee

Atty. Jesus Melchor V. Quitain

Atty. Quitain is a seasoned lawyer and public servant who served as Chief Presidential Legal Counsel and Special Assistant to the President. He is recognized as the principal speechwriter for many of President Duterte’s major addresses.

He earned his law degree from San Beda College and served as Dean of the College of Law at the University of Mindanao. His distinguished career includes roles as City Legal Officer, City Administrator, and Resident Ombudsman of Davao City.

Trustee

Atty. Leocadio S. Nitorreda

Leocadio Nitorreda is a seasoned corporate executive and lawyer with extensive experience in governance and compliance. He holds a degree in Business Management from Ateneo de Manila and a Bachelor of Laws from Ateneo de Davao.

He holds board memberships in several major corporations, including USSC, NADECOR, and the JVA Group. He has been actively involved in advancing UM’s institutional governance since 2005.

Trustee

Atty. Jesus Dureza

Jesus "Jess" Dureza is a veteran lawyer, journalist, and public servant. He served as Presidential Adviser on the Peace Process and Chairman of the Mindanao Development Authority. He holds a Bachelor of Laws from Ateneo de Davao, placing 10th in the 1973 Bar Exams.

His extensive career spans journalism, peace negotiations, and various cabinet-level positions under multiple Philippine presidents. He has been a member of the UM Board since 2011.

Trustee

Darlene Magnolia Custodio

Darlene Magnolia Antonino-Custodio is a former public servant who served as Mayor of General Santos City and Representative of South Cotabato. She earned her business degree from De La Salle University and a Master's in Culinary Arts from Le Cordon Bleu, London.

Coming from a prominent political family, she has held key legislative roles, including House Deputy Minority Leader. She has served as an UM Trustee since 2010.

About UM

The University of Mindanao is a private, non-sectarian higher education institution in Davao City, Philippines.
