Academic Departments

Empowering the next generation of professionals.

Accounting Education

Architecture & Fine Arts

First to offer Architecture in Davao. PACUCOA Level IV accredited programs.

Arts & Sciences Education

Most diverse academic unit. Cultivates creative thought, strong intellectual inquiry, and high academic responsibility.

Basic Education

Expansive facilities for Preschool to Senior High. Centers for learning and play in Matina, Tibungco, and Tagum.

Business Administration

Center of Excellence by CHED and Level IV PACUCOA accredited. Prepares individuals for global success.

Computing Education

PACUCOA Level IV accredited and Center of Development. Partnered with Apple, Google, and Microsoft.

Criminal Justice

Premier Criminology school. Center of Excellence with Level IV PACUCOA accreditation.

Engineering Education

Premier engineering school. Exclusive representation of Mindanao in the ASEAN AIMS Program.

Health Sciences

Strong nursing and med-tech programs with established hospital linkages for hands-on learning.

Hospitality Education

Equips professionals for the tourism industry. PACUCOA Level III accredited programs.

Legal Education

Adopts creative learning with the UM Legal Advocacy Network (ULAN) extension program.

Professional Schools

Teacher Education

Center of Excellence and Center of Teacher Training. Consistently produces top LET board placers.

Technical School

Enhances employability through industry partnerships and TESDA certification programs.
